About me

Hi, I’m Han! I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Nanjing University and a research intern in the Data, Knowledge, and Intelligence (DKI) group at Microsoft Research Asia, Beijing.

I also work with Prof. He Ye at University College London and Prof. Jiaheng Liu at Nanjing University’s LINK Lab.

My research focuses on AI for Software Engineering, especially coding agents and agentic reinforcement learning. I study how agents retrieve context, navigate repositories, and learn from execution. I build benchmarks and analysis tools to make their behavior more measurable, interpretable, and reliable.

Let’s connect. If you’re interested in coding agents, evaluation, or agentic learning, I’d be happy to chat. Reach me at 231220161@smail.nju.edu.cn.
